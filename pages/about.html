Our Farm & Heritage

Real Dairy, Raised With Love & Care

MilkyWay Farms (Chanumolu Harsha Vardhan) is a dedicated local dairy farm founded with a single mission: to bring wholesome, untouched, and unadulterated dairy straight from our green pastures to your breakfast table.

What Sets Us Apart

In a world of ultra-processed packaged milk and chemical additives, we believe in keeping dairy simple, natural, and transparent. We treat our cattle with respect, ensuring they roam freely and feed on nutrient-rich green fodder.

🌱 100% Organic Fodder

Our cattle are fed clean, pesticide-free grass and mineral-enriched grain mixes.

🔬 Zero Hormones or Oxytocin

We strictly prohibit synthetic hormones, antibiotics, or milk yield enhancers.

❄️ Immediate 4°C Chilling

Milk is chilled within 45 minutes of milking to stop bacterial growth naturally.

🍶 Eco-Friendly Glass Bottles

Delivered in sanitised, recyclable glass bottles to prevent plastic micro-contamination.

Traditional Handcrafted Dairy

Beyond our fresh daily milk, our dairy products are made using timeless artisanal methods:

  • Desi Bilona Ghee: Prepared by bi-directional churning of whole milk curd, preserving vital A2 beta-casein proteins and golden aroma.
  • Natural Earthen Matka Curd: Allowed to ferment naturally in porous clay pots for thick, rich, gut-healing probiotic texture.
  • Fresh Malai Paneer: Crafted by gentle natural lemon/whey curdling for unmatched softness and high protein.
