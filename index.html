Our Hygiene Promise

From Our Farm to Your Cup in 3 Hours

We believe good food starts with ethical farming, clean processes, and honest relationships.

1

Pasture Grazing

Our cattle graze freely on organic green fodder and pesticide-free pasture, ensuring nutrient-dense milk.

2

Touchless Milking

Hygienic automated milking with direct sanitised stainless steel lines ensures zero human contact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our morning delivery slot runs between 5:30 AM and 7:00 AM every day so you always have fresh milk ready for your morning routine. We also offer an evening slot between 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM upon request.
Our milk is fresh, raw, and rapidly chilled to 4°C immediately after milking. It contains no preservatives, stabilizers, or added milk powder. We recommend boiling it once before consumption.
You can pause, resume, or change your daily quantity anytime via our subscription page or by messaging us directly on WhatsApp at least 12 hours in advance.
For individual orders, we accept Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI upon delivery. For monthly subscriptions, a consolidated monthly statement is sent for easy settlement.
